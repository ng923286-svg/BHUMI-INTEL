Policy Brief: Protecting agricultural land at the urban edge

A concise evidence-to-policy synthesis for the Haryana growth corridor.

1. Problem

Rapid urban expansion is placing pressure on agricultural land around high-growth corridors.

2. Evidence & key findings

Linked research, sample land-use metadata and regional indicators point to a concentrated conversion signal around Gurugram.

3. Possible interventions

Strengthen land-use monitoring, focus protection on productive edge zones and align infrastructure review with agricultural land indicators.

4. Risks & expected impact

Implementation depends on district-level validation. The scenario model estimates a directional reduction in conversion pressure—not a guaranteed outcome.

5. Sources

Urban Growth & Agricultural Land · Sustainable Land Use Policy · Land Use / Land Cover · Gurugram regional evidence.
